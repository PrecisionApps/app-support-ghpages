Inklands · Support

Draw the map your world deserves.

Inklands is a fantasy map maker for iPad and Apple Pencil: coasts, mountains, forests, rivers, towns and hand-lettered names, each one still editable, on a sheet you can print.

Get help

Email makstechnologyglobal@gmail.com. Include your iPad model and iPadOS version, and what you expected to happen. You'll normally hear back within two working days.

How do I start a map?

Tap New map, choose World, Region, City or Battle map, and name it. Pick the coast pen and paint the land; the coastline inks itself around what you paint.

How do I add mountains, forests, rivers and towns?

Each has its own instrument in the strip on the left: the range brush for mountains, hills and volcanoes, the terrain brush for forest, marsh, dunes, snow and grassland, the line pen for rivers, roads and borders, and stamps for hamlets, towns, cities and capitals. Tap an instrument twice to see its options.

How do I write a name along a curve?

Choose the label quill and draw the path the name should follow, then type it. Tap instead of drawing for a straight label. Tap a label later to edit or move it.

Can I change something after I drew it?

Yes. Every range, river, town and name is an object in its own layer. Select the instrument that made it, tap the object, and move it, change its kind, rename it or delete it. Layers can be hidden and reordered in the legend.

How do I add a frame, compass, title plate or grid?

Choose the frame and grid instrument and tap the map. The panel sets the neat line, the compass rose, the title plate, a hex or square grid and the scale bar.

How do I export or print?

Tap the share button at the top right of a map and choose PNG or PDF. Free exports up to 2048 pixels on the long side with a small "Made with Inklands" line; Inklands Pro exports the full sheet, clean.

Where are my maps kept?

On your iPad, as files. You can move them to iCloud Drive in the Files app. Deleting the app removes the maps stored inside it.

How do I restore Inklands Pro on a new iPad?

Open the gallery, tap the colophon button at the top right, and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel?

In the same sheet choose Manage subscription, or open iPadOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
