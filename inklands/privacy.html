Inklands · Privacy Policy

Your maps stay on your iPad.

Your maps

Maps are files on your iPad, or in your own iCloud Drive if you move them there. They are never uploaded to us and we cannot see them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. To know whether Inklands Pro is active, the app shares an anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app with RevenueCat, our subscription service. It is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or your maps. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app may schedule reminders on your iPad about an offer. They are created on the device; no message is sent from a server, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no analytics of what you draw.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is published at this address and in the app.
