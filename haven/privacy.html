Privacy Policy

Haven — a private space for the people who matter most

The short version

Haven is private by construction. Your memories live on your device and in your own iCloud — we run no servers, we cannot read your content, and we collect nothing about you. There are no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs in the app.

What Haven stores, and where

  • Your memories — capsules, Vault entries, glows, photos, and voice notes are stored locally on your device.
  • iCloud sync — if iCloud is available, Haven syncs your haven through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. The people you invite access it through Apple's private sharing. We operate no server of our own and have no ability to access, read, or decrypt what you keep in your haven.
  • Invite codes — when you invite someone, Haven publishes a minimal lookup record (the six-character code mapped to an Apple sharing link). It contains no memory content and no personal information.

Photos and microphone

Photos you pick and voice notes you record become part of your capsules — nothing else. They stay on your device and in your private iCloud. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Notifications

Haven sends gentle local notifications — when a sealed capsule is ready to open, when something warm arrives from your circle, and occasional reminders you can ignore. Remote push signals are used only to let your device know your iCloud data changed; they carry no content we can read.

Analytics and tracking

None. Haven contains no third-party analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind.

Purchases and subscriptions

Billing is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. To manage or refund a subscription, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or your Apple ID subscription settings.

Your data, your control

  • You can export a complete backup of your haven from inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.
  • Data synced to iCloud belongs to your Apple account and can be managed through iCloud settings.

Children's privacy

Haven is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in any material way, the change will be reflected on this page.
