Common questions

How do I invite someone? Open the Circle tab and share your six-character invite code. A haven holds up to four people.

Where are my memories stored? On your device, and — when iCloud is available — in your own private iCloud. We run no servers and can never read your content.

How do I back up my haven? In the Circle tab, under “Keeping your memories,” you can export a complete backup file and restore it on any device.

How do I manage my subscription? Subscriptions are billed by Apple — manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
