Glidepath · Support

Always know where you are in your week.

Glidepath logs your GLP-1 shots, rotates your injection sites, estimates your medication level hour by hour, and shows which days side effects and food noise come back.

Get help

Email makstechnologyglobal@gmail.com. Include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and what you expected to happen. You'll normally hear back within two working days.

How is my medication level estimated?

Glidepath draws the curve from the doses you log and the published half-life of your medication. It is an estimate of where you are in your week, not a blood measurement, and it never tells you what dose to take.

I took my shot late, or on a different day.

Log the shot with the date and time you actually took it. Your curve, your next shot day and your reminders follow the shot you logged.

How do I change my medication, dose or shot day?

Open Settings → Your plan. Shots you already logged keep the dose you logged them with.

My reminders aren't arriving.

Open Settings → Reminders. If you see “Notifications are off for Glidepath”, tap Open iOS Settings and allow notifications, then turn the reminders you want back on.

How does Apple Health sync work?

Turn on Settings → Apple Health → Sync weight with Apple Health. Weights you log are saved to Health, and weights from Health are added to your trend. Glidepath reads and writes Weight only.

How do I get the report for my doctor?

Open Trends and tap Report for your doctor. It builds a PDF of your shots, weight trend and side effects that you can share or print. The report is part of the Glidepath plan.

How do I restore my plan on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Your data → Delete all logs removes every entry from this device. Deleting the app removes everything Glidepath stored.
