Glidepath · Privacy Policy

Your logs stay on your device.

What Glidepath stores

The medication, dose and schedule you choose, and everything you log: shots, injection sites, notes, weights, side effects, protein and water. All of it is saved on this device. Glidepath has no account system and does not upload your logs to a server.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health sync, Glidepath reads and writes Weight only. Health data stays on your device under Apple's protections. It is never used for advertising and never shared with anyone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Glidepath uses RevenueCat to confirm that a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app — not your name, your logs or your health data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on this device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Exports

A report or CSV is created only when you ask for one and goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all logs removes every entry from this device. Deleting the app removes everything Glidepath stored. Weights saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you remove them there.

Children

Glidepath is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with an app update and its date changes below.
