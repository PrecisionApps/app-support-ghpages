Placard · Support

One photo. A week of ads.

Placard turns a photo of your product into studio photoshoots, ad creatives in every size, video ads and UGC videos.

Get help

Email makstechnologyglobal@gmail.com. Include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and what you expected to happen. You'll normally hear back within two working days.

How do I start?

Add a product photo — any phone photo works. Placard cuts the product out on your iPhone, then shoots it in new scenes, writes ad copy, and makes ads and videos from it. Open Create and pick what to make.

What does a photoshoot do?

It keeps your product exactly as photographed and puts it in a new scene: a studio sweep, travertine in the sun, a beach, holiday lights, or one you describe. A photoshoot takes about 30 seconds and uses 2 credits.

How do I make an ad in every size?

Open Ad creatives, pick a headline from the copywriter or write your own, choose a layout and what sits behind the product, then Export. Pick any of Story & Reel (9:16), Feed (4:5), Square, Landscape and Link banner, and save them all to Photos at once. Designing ads is free.

How do video ads work?

Choose a style: Motion ad (5 or 10 seconds), Cinematic (8 seconds with sound), Commercial (five cuts with sound) or UGC creator (an AI creator presents your product). The video is rendered by our studio in one to four minutes; your iPhone then adds your hook, headline, button, voiceover and an end card. You can change the words, the voice and the size later for free.

Who are the UGC creators?

AI-generated people. No real person's likeness is used. Please don't upload photos of other people as products.

What are credits?

Generations cost credits: photoshoots and edits 2, motion ads 10–20, cinematic 45, commercial 50, UGC 120. Your plan adds credits every week (weekly) or every month (yearly), and a free trial includes 30. Credit packs never expire. A video that fails to render gives its credits back.

Why is there a “Made with Placard” tag?

Exports without a plan carry a small tag. With a plan, exports are clean — and you can remove the tag from anything you made before, from its page in your Library.

My video is taking a while.

It keeps rendering if you leave the app and appears in your Library when it's ready. If notifications are on, Placard tells you. If a render fails, its credits come back automatically.

How do I restore my plan on a new iPhone?

Open Brand → Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with. Your credits follow your plan.

How do I cancel?

Open Brand → Settings → Manage subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I delete my work?

Brand → Settings → Delete everything removes every product, photoshoot, ad and video from your iPhone. Copies you saved to Photos stay there.
