Placard · Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

In short

Placard turns photos of your products into ads. To do that, the photos and words you choose to generate from are sent to Placard's studio server and the AI services it uses. Placard has no accounts, shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your iPhone

Your brand kit, product photos, cut-outs, and every photoshoot, ad and video you make are stored on your device and included in your own device backups. Product cut-outs and the final type, voice and end card on videos are made on your iPhone.

What is sent to make something

When you ask for a photoshoot, an edit, ad copy, a product description, a voiceover or a video, Placard sends what that request needs — the product photo or scene, your product's name, price and description, your brand name and voice, and the direction you typed — to Placard's studio server (Google Cloud, United States). The server passes it to OpenAI (images, text and voice) or Runway (video) to produce the result, and keeps a generated photoshoot for up to 7 days so it can be fetched again; videos are fetched from Runway, whose links expire within two days. Each service handles that data under its own API terms; OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default. Nothing is sent until you ask for a generation.

AI creators

UGC videos are presented by AI-generated people. No real person's likeness is used. Do not upload photos of other people as products.

Purchases and credits

Subscriptions and credit packs are handled by Apple. RevenueCat tells Placard whether your plan is active, using an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. Placard's server uses that same anonymous ID to keep your credit balance. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

Usage statistics

Placard records anonymous usage events — for example that a photoshoot was started or an ad was exported, and which screens were opened — to improve the app. These never include your photos, product details, ad copy or prompts.

Notifications

If you allow them, Placard schedules notifications on your device: when a video you started should be ready, a reminder before a free trial ends, and occasional offers. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Your content

You own the photos you add and the ads Placard makes from them. You are responsible for having the right to use the product photos, brand names and claims in your ads. “Delete everything” in Settings removes all of it from this iPhone.

Children

Placard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
