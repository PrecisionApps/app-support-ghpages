Ridgewise · Privacy Policy

What you can see is worked out on your device.

The short version

Ridgewise works out which peaks you can see on your device. It has no accounts, shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your location

Your position is used on your device to compute the horizon. It is not sent to us. To draw the terrain, Ridgewise downloads elevation tiles for the area around the viewpoint from a public map-data host (Amazon Web Services); like any download, that request reveals the rough area and your IP address to the host. Place search and place names use Apple Maps.

Camera and photos

The camera image is shown on screen so names can be pinned on it and is never recorded or uploaded. A photo you choose is read on your device, including its location and direction data, and stays there. Pictures you export are saved or shared only where you send them.

Your logbook and settings

The summits you log and your preferences are stored on your device and are included in your device backups.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To know whether your plan is active, Ridgewise uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, Ridgewise schedules notifications on your device: the alpenglow alert you can switch on, a reminder before a free trial ends, and occasional offers. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Children

Ridgewise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app.
