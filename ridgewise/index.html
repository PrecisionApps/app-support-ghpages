Ridgewise · Support

Every mountain on your horizon, named.

Ridgewise works out which peaks you can see from where you stand and puts a name, an elevation and a distance on each one: on a drawn horizon, over your camera, or in a photo you already took.

Get help

Email makstechnologyglobal@gmail.com. Include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, where you were standing, and what you expected to see. You'll normally hear back within two working days.

A peak is labelled in the wrong place, or the names are shifted.

The compass in your phone is the usual cause. Move away from metal and cars, wave the phone in a figure of eight, then drag the horizon sideways until the drawn ridgeline sits on the real one. Ridgewise keeps that correction until you move on.

A mountain I can see has no name.

Peak names and positions come from open geographic data, which is thinner in some regions. Free use names the peaks near you; Ridgewise Pro names every peak to the horizon.

Does it work without signal?

The peak database is on your phone. The terrain that hides and reveals peaks is downloaded for the area you are in; use Download terrain in Settings before you leave coverage. Without terrain, Ridgewise still names peaks but cannot tell which ones are hidden behind a nearer ridge, and says so on screen.

How do I identify the mountains in a photo?

Tap the photo button on the View tab and choose a picture. Ridgewise uses the place and direction saved in the photo. If the photo has no location, set the viewpoint on the map, then drag and pinch until the white trace sits on the skyline.

How do I see the view from somewhere else?

Tap the pin button on the View tab, search for a place or drop a pin, and Ridgewise draws the horizon from there.

Ridgewise can't find my location.

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Ridgewise and allow While Using the App with Precise Location on. You can always choose a place by hand instead.

How do I switch between feet and metres?

Open Settings → Units.

How do I restore my plan on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings → Manage subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Where does the data come from?

Peaks: GeoNames, licensed CC BY 4.0. Terrain: the Terrain Tiles open dataset hosted by Amazon Web Services. Maps and place search: Apple Maps. Full credits are in Settings → Data sources.
