Solstice · Privacy Policy

Everything you log stays on your device.

The short version

Everything you log in Solstice stays on your iPhone. Solstice has no accounts, shows no ads, has no server of its own and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your health record

Hot flashes, check-ins, symptoms, bleeding, treatments, doses and notes are stored on your device and are included in your own device backups. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The developer cannot see them.

Reports and exports

The doctor report and the spreadsheet export are created on your device. They leave it only when you choose to send, save or print them, and only to where you send them.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the Log a Hot Flash shortcut, the entry is saved on your device like any other. Requests you speak to Siri are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To know whether your plan is active, Solstice uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. It receives nothing you log. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, Solstice schedules notifications on your device: the daily check-in reminder and treatment reminders you switch on, a reminder before a free trial ends, and occasional offers. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete everything I've logged removes the whole record from this device at once. Deleting the app does the same.

Not medical advice

Solstice is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Decisions about your health and treatment belong with you and your doctor.

Children

Solstice is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, and the date below changes.
