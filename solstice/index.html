Your whole day on one ring.

Solstice logs hot flashes in one tap, keeps a ten-second daily check-in of sleep, mood and symptoms, tracks HRT and other treatments, and builds a one-page report for your doctor.

Get help

Email makstechnologyglobal@gmail.com. Include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and what you expected to happen. You'll normally hear back within two working days.

How do I log a hot flash?

Open Today and tap Log a hot flash. It is saved at once with the current time. The sheet that follows lets you add how strong it was, what came just before it, and whether it woke you. You can also say “Log a hot flash in Solstice” to Siri, or put the Log a Hot Flash shortcut on your Action button.

I forgot to log one. Can I add it later?

Yes. Log it now, then change the date and time under When in the sheet that follows. To correct an older entry, tap it in Today or in Patterns → history.

What is the daily check-in?

A ten-second record of last night's sleep, your mood, any of 23 symptoms and bleeding. Tap a symptom once to add it, again for stronger, and once more to clear it. Answer what applies and skip the rest.

How does “Is it working?” work?

For each treatment, Solstice compares your average hot flashes a day in the two weeks before you started with the latest two weeks. It is a count from your own log. Other things may have changed in that time too, so bring it to your doctor rather than reading it as proof.

How do I get the report for my doctor?

Open Patterns and tap Doctor report. Choose 30 or 90 days, then Send or print. The report is created on your device and is part of the Solstice plan.

My reminders aren't arriving.

Open Settings → Reminders and check that the daily check-in is on and has a time. Treatment reminders are set on each treatment. If notifications are off for Solstice in iOS Settings, allow them there first.

How do I restore my plan on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings → Manage subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Your data → Export everything as a spreadsheet writes a CSV you can save or send. Delete everything I've logged removes the whole record from this device at once. Deleting the app does the same.
