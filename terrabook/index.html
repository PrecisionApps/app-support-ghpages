Terrabook · Support

Every country in your pocket.

Terrabook is an offline atlas and factbook of 250 countries and territories, with four ways to learn them: flags, capitals, shapes and the map.

Get help

Email makstechnologyglobal@gmail.com. Include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and what you expected to happen. You'll normally hear back within two working days.

A figure looks out of date.

Chapter text and figures come from The World Factbook, a public-domain reference that is no longer updated. Terrabook shows the year each figure dates from next to it, so you can judge how current it is.

A country or territory has no chapters.

A few territories have no entry of their own in the Factbook. Terrabook shows the facts that exist for them rather than leaving blanks.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every country, figure, chapter, outline and flag ships inside the app. Only the live Apple Maps view on a country page needs a connection.

How does the daily round work?

Each round brings back the ones you missed before adding new ones. Finish a round each day to keep your streak. You can practise the whole world or one continent.

How do I mark where I have been?

Open a country and tap the pin, or the star for a favourite. My World shows them on your map.

How do I start over?

Settings → Erase marks and progress removes everything you have marked and learned from this device.

How do I restore my plan on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings → Manage subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Where does the data come from?

Chapters and figures: The World Factbook (public domain). Country records and outlines: the mledoze/countries dataset, made available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Some outlines: Natural Earth (public domain). Flags: Flagpedia. Full credits are in Settings → Sources and licences.
