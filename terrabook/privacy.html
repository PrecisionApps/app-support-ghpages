Terrabook · Privacy Policy

What you mark and learn stays on your device.

What Terrabook stores

Your home country, your interests, the countries you mark as favourites, visited or wanted, and your quiz history. All of it is saved on this device. Terrabook has no account system and does not upload any of it to a server.

The atlas itself

Every country, figure, chapter, outline and flag ships inside the app. Reading them sends nothing anywhere.

Apple Maps

The live map on an entry is drawn by Apple Maps, which contacts Apple to load map tiles for the area shown. Terrabook does not ask for or use your location.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Terrabook uses RevenueCat to confirm that a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app — not your name, your marks or your quiz history.

Notifications

The daily round reminder and offer reminders are scheduled on this device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Sharing

A fact card is created only when you ask for one and goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase marks and progress removes everything you have marked and learned from this device. Deleting the app removes everything Terrabook stored.

Children

Terrabook is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with an app update and its date changes below.
